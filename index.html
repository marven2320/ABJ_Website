Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what clients most often ask before starting a project.

What services does AB&J Engineering Works offer?

We provide electrical engineering services (PLC design, programming, and SCADA), load planning and optimization software for generation plants, IoT-based smart products, and PV estimate and installation for solar projects. See our full Products & Services page for details.

Where is AB&J Engineering Works located, and what areas do you serve?

We're based at Tulip St., Brgy. Tubod, Iligan City, and we work with utilities, institutions, and industry partners across Mindanao. Projects outside the region can be discussed on a case-by-case basis — get in touch through our Contact Us page.

How do I request a quotation?

Every service on our Products & Services page has a "Request a quotation" button that takes you to our contact form with that service pre-selected. You can also reach us directly by phone or email — details are on the Contact Us page.

What is the typical process for a PV installation project?

We start with a site assessment and load study, move into system design and sizing, issue a formal proposal and quotation, then handle permitting support, installation, and testing and commissioning. Full details and an indicative cost calculator are on our PV Estimate and Installation page.

Does AB&J assist with net-metering applications?

Yes. For grid-tied systems, we assist clients with the documentation and utility coordination needed for net-metering applications as part of the installation process.

What warranties come with a PV installation?

Typical coverage includes a manufacturer's panel performance warranty, an inverter warranty, and a workmanship warranty from AB&J. Exact terms depend on the equipment specified for your project — see the PV Estimate and Installation page for standard ranges.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary with scope — a small PV installation may take a few weeks from proposal to commissioning, while monitoring and control system projects for utilities are scheduled around plant outage windows. We'll confirm a timeline once we understand your project.

Do you work with government and institutional clients?

Yes. We have completed and ongoing projects with the National Power Corporation, MSU-Iligan Institute of Technology, Bacolod Water District, and GNPower Kauswagan Ltd. Co.
