Indicative planning estimate only, based on typical Mindanao solar irradiance (3.8–4.6 peak sun hours/day) and standard turnkey pricing ranges. Actual system design, cost, and generation depend on roof condition, shading, electrical setup, and site assessment findings.

Labor cost adjustment. The labor portion of this estimate is carried at 1.3× our baseline installation rates to reflect current inflation in construction wages, mobilization, and consumables. Labor is assumed at 20% of the turnkey rate for residential, 15% for commercial, and 18% for hybrid systems; equipment and balance-of-system costs are held at baseline. The uplift applies to labor only, not to the full contract price.

Location limitations. These rates assume a site within our regular service radius in Northern Mindanao and the Lanao provinces, reachable by road, with standard single- or two-storey roof access. Costs will exceed the range shown for sites that are off-road or require specialized access equipment; located on islands or in areas requiring ferry or barge freight for panels, inverters, and batteries; situated far enough from our base to require crew lodging and extended mobilization; subject to local permit, right-of-way, or utility interconnection requirements beyond the standard distribution-utility process; or affected by terrain, elevation, or local shading that reduces usable irradiance below the Mindanao averages used here. A firm price for any such location is issued only after an on-site assessment.

Our installation process

A consistent sequence from first assessment to a commissioned, warrantied system.

1

Site assessment & load study

We review roof or ground space, shading, structural condition, and your electrical load profile.

2

System design & sizing

We size panels, inverter, and (if applicable) battery capacity to match your load and site conditions.

3

Proposal & quotation

A formal quotation with equipment specification, cost, and project timeline for your approval.

4

Permitting support

Assistance with utility coordination and net-metering documentation for grid-tied systems.

5

Installation

Mounting, wiring, and integration carried out by our engineering team on-site.

6

Testing & commissioning

Full system testing and performance verification before energizing and handover.

7

Handover & training

A walkthrough of your system and monitoring tools so you know how it performs day to day.

8

Warranty & maintenance

Ongoing support and maintenance options for the life of your system.
